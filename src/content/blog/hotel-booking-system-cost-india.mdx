Hotel owners in Vizag and across India often ask for a price before they ask for a product. That is fair. A booking system can mean a WhatsApp reply tool, a room calendar, or a full hotel package.

Here is a simple way to think about cost, from a software company that builds hotel tools in Visakhapatnam.

## What most small hotels need first

Before a big system, most front desks need three things:

1. A fast reply to room enquiries on WhatsApp or the website.
2. One room list so the same room is not promised twice.
3. A confirmation message with check-in time and the advance paid.

If those three work, the hotel stops losing weekend bookings to slow replies.

## What changes the price

Cost rises when you add:

- online advance payments,
- connection to Booking.com or MakeMyTrip,
- several properties under one owner login,
- restaurant or banquet bookings in the same system,
- guest feedback after check-out.

A 12-room property that only needs WhatsApp availability and a desk calendar is a smaller project than a multi-property setup with channel managers.

## A sensible budget conversation

Ask any software company in Vizag for a written first version:

- which messages are automated,
- who confirms the booking, the assistant or the desk,
- whether room stock is live or updated by hand,
- monthly WhatsApp or hosting costs after launch.

Avoid a quote that only says "hotel software" with no list of screens.

## Start with one busy channel

If most enquiries arrive on WhatsApp at night, fix that channel first. Add the website booking form later. Hotels that try to automate every channel on day one usually pay more and use less.
