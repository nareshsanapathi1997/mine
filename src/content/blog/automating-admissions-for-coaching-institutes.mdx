In a busy admission season, a coaching centre gets enquiries from everywhere: Instagram ads, Google, walk-ins, phone calls and WhatsApp. Most centres do not lose students because of poor teaching. They lose them because an enquiry was not followed up in time.

Here is a simple order of work to fix that. You do not need to do it all at once.

## Step 1: Put every enquiry in one list

Before any AI or automation, make sure every enquiry lands in one place, with:

- the student's name and phone number,
- the course they asked about (JEE, NEET, foundation, etc.),
- where the enquiry came from,
- the counsellor responsible, and
- the next follow-up date.

This alone stops many enquiries slipping through.

## Step 2: Answer common questions instantly

Most first messages are the same: fees, batch timings, location, demo class. A WhatsApp assistant can answer these at any hour, using only the fee and batch details you give it. It also saves the enquiry into your list.

What it should not do: promise ranks, offer discounts, or try to counsel a worried parent. Those conversations belong to your counsellors.

## Step 3: Book demo classes on their own

If a student wants a demo, the assistant can show open demo slots, book one, and send a reminder the day before. The counsellor sees the booking and gets a reminder to call after the demo.

## Step 4: Make fee instalments easy

Once a student joins, set up the instalment plan in your software. Reminders with a payment link go out a few days before each due date. Paid instalments are marked automatically and a receipt is sent.

## Step 5: Keep parents informed

Batch changes, test schedules and results can go out on WhatsApp from your official number, instead of from teachers' personal phones.

## What stays with people

Software can save, remind and answer routine questions. It cannot understand a family's worries or decide which course suits a child. Keep your counsellors for that, and let software handle the follow-ups they never have time for.

Start with step 1. If you only do that well, you will already see fewer missed enquiries.
