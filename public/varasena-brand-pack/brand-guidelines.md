# VARASENA Technologies — Brand Pack

## Brand direction
Premium technology identity: glowing cyan–blue ribbon V, bold VARASENA wordmark with cyan A accents, and the line Ideas | Innovation | Impact.

## Colors
- Deep navy: `#071B45`
- Electric blue: `#0B6BFF`
- Cyan: `#00D4E8`
- Light background: `#F4F8FF`
- White: `#FFFFFF`

## Tagline
Ideas | Innovation | Impact

## Included files (used by the site)
- `varasena-logo-light.png` — horizontal logo for light backgrounds
- `varasena-logo-dark.png` — horizontal logo for dark backgrounds (transparent)
- `varasena-icon-clear.png` — standalone V symbol (transparent, favicon / mobile)
- `varasena-icon-hero.png` — larger premium V for the home hero
- `varasena-logo.svg` / `varasena-logo-dark-background.svg` / `varasena-icon.svg` / `varasena-icon-dark-background.svg` — vector sources

## Usage
- Prefer the horizontal logo in the site header and footer.
- Use the standalone symbol for favicon, app icon, and compact mobile navigation.
- Leave clear space around the mark at least equal to 1/4 of the icon width.
- Do not stretch, skew, or recolor the artwork.
